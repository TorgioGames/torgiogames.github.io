Write to us

For questions, ideas or problems: torgiogames@gmail.com. If you are reporting a bug, please include your phone model, what happened and, if you can, a screenshot. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked

How do I play? Drag your jelly friends with your finger to the home of their own colour. Same colours merge and give you extra time. Clear the board before the clock runs out.

Where is my progress saved? On your phone; it is not sent to us. If your phone's own backup is on, your progress moves with you to a new phone.

Do I need the internet? No, the game plays offline.

How do I turn off play statistics? Settings › Share play statistics.

How do I change the language? Settings › Language. The game is in 6 languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), German and French.
