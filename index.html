Lanterns of Vela · Son Fenerci

A calm, story-rich colour puzzle. Carry glowing light to the lanterns, relight a dark harbour town street by street, and uncover what really happened on the night the lighthouse went dark. 240 levels, 24 hand-painted memories, weekly tasks, no lives, no timers, no forced ads, plays offline.
