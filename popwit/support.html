Frequently asked

How do I cancel my subscription?
Android: Google Play › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Popwit. iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Popwit.

I moved to a new phone and Plus is missing.
Tap Settings › Restore purchases in the app while signed in to the same store account.

I find some colors hard to tell apart.
Settings › Shape mode plays Ink Trick with shapes instead of colors. The other games use shapes and numbers as well as color.

My streak was reset.
Your streak grows every day you finish the daily set and resets if you miss a day; the Popwit Plus streak shield covers one day a week.
