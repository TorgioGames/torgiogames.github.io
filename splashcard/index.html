KARAKÖY · SEPTEMBER 197416:00

Tap the girl sketching, the gulls, the cat or the ferry · drag the sky

It all began on a ferry. Karaköy, September 1974: a gust of wind, a sketch in the air. Half a century later, a granddaughter finds an album full of postcards that were never posted.

Find the drops

Little paint drops hide in every faded postcard. Every one you find paints the picture back to life.

Türkiye, city by city

From Istanbul to Mardin, every city is a hand-painted postcard of its landmark.

No rush

No timer. Hints are there when you are stuck, and the notes on the back of the cards tell the story piece by piece.
