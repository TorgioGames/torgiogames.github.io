Write to us

For questions, ideas or problems: tprkglhn02@gmail.com. If you are reporting a bug, please include your phone model, what happened and, if you can, a screenshot. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked

Where is my progress saved? On your phone; it is not sent to us. If your phone's own backup is on, your progress moves with you to a new phone.

Do I need the internet? No. The game plays offline; the internet is used only for the purchase and crash reports (and statistics, if you allowed them).

Are there ads? No. Cases 1 and 2 are free; cases 3–5 unlock with one purchase. On a new phone: Settings › Restore purchase.

How do I switch off statistics? Settings › Share play statistics.

How do I change the language? Settings › Language. The game is in Turkish and English.
