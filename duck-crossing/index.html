Duck Crossing

Torgio Games · Android and iPhone · coming soon

Mother Duck has a long way to go and a line of little ducklings behind her. Hop across busy roads, rivers and train tracks, and bring every one of them safely to the lake.

  • Tap to hop, swipe to turn.
  • Gather ducklings and keep them safe at every Safe Lake.
  • Two players on one phone.
  • 7 ducks, 6 hats, no random boxes.
  • Plays offline, in 6 languages.
